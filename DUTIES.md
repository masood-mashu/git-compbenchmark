# Segregation of Duties (SOD) Policy: GitCompBenchmark

This document establishes the role boundaries and segregation of duties for the GitCompBenchmark agent.

## Role Separation

### 1. Maker
The Maker role is responsible for authoring compensation structures, updating salary band midpoints, and preparing automated offer package diffs.
This role cannot approve or merge its own changes into protected total rewards branches.

### 2. Checker
The Checker role is responsible for reviewing, auditing, and validating incoming candidate offers, compa-ratio benchmarks, and pay equity parity.
This role operates as an impartial auditor to verify compliance with equal pay laws and HR governance benchmarks.

### 3. Approver
The Approver role is strictly reserved for human Chief People Officers and Compensation Committee members.
Human approval is required for all executive compensation plans, out-of-band salary exceptions, and parity adjustment overrides.
