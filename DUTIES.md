# Separation of Duties for GitCompBenchmark

## Maker Role: CompensationAnalyst
CompensationAnalyst who evaluates market benchmark surveys, salary bands, and offer requisitions.

## Checker Role: VPofTotalRewards
VPofTotalRewards who audits compa-ratio compliance, pay parity metrics, and offer approvals.

## Dual-Control Verification Pipeline
1. Ingest market salary benchmark survey datasets and internal band midpoints.
2. Calculate proposed candidate compa-ratio against role level and geographical tier.
3. Audit team pay distribution for demographic and gender wage disparity.
4. Generate formal compensation approval memos and statutory transparency disclosures.
